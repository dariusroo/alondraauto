Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 7, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Alondra Auto Care ("we," "us," or "our") handles information in connection with our website at www.alondraauto.com (the "Website"). This policy is provided in accordance with the California Online Privacy Protection Act (CalOPPA) and other applicable California law.

Information We Collect

We do not collect any personal information through this Website. The Website is an informational page only. It has no contact forms, account registration, online payments, newsletter sign-ups, or chat features, and we do not use cookies, analytics, advertising pixels, or other tracking technologies.

If you choose to call us, email us, or visit the shop, any information you share (such as your name, phone number, or vehicle details) is collected by us offline for the purpose of providing the service you request, and is not collected through the Website.

Third-Party Services

Although we do not collect information ourselves, the following third-party services are used to operate the Website and may process limited technical information under their own privacy policies:

  • GitHub Pages (website hosting). The Website is hosted by GitHub, Inc. When you visit, GitHub may automatically log technical information such as your IP address for security and operational purposes. See the GitHub General Privacy Statement (opens in a new tab).
  • Google Maps (embedded map and directions links). The Website displays an embedded Google Map and links to Google Maps for directions. When the map loads or you click a directions link, Google may collect information such as your IP address and may set cookies. See the Google Privacy Policy (opens in a new tab).

We do not control, and are not responsible for, the privacy practices of these third parties. Links on the Website to other sites are governed by those sites' policies.

Sale or Sharing of Personal Information

We do not sell or share personal information, including for cross-context behavioral advertising, and have not done so in the preceding 12 months. We do not disclose personal information to third parties for their direct marketing purposes (California Civil Code § 1798.83, "Shine the Light").

Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control

Because the Website does not track visitors over time or across other websites, there is no tracking for a "Do Not Track" browser signal or a Global Privacy Control signal to turn off. Third-party services described above, such as Google Maps, may handle these signals according to their own policies.

Your California Privacy Rights

California residents may have rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), as amended by the California Privacy Rights Act (CPRA), including the right to know what personal information a business collects, to request deletion or correction of that information, to opt out of its sale or sharing, and to not be discriminated against for exercising these rights. Because the Website collects no personal information, we hold no Website data about you. If you have questions about information you have shared with the shop directly, contact us using the details below and we will respond as required by law.

Children's Privacy

The Website is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children through the Website.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a new effective date. Because we do not collect contact information through the Website, posting on this page is how we notify visitors of changes.
