Accessibility Statement

Last reviewed: October 7, 2026

Alondra Auto Care is committed to making our website at www.alondraauto.com usable by everyone, including people with disabilities. We want every customer to be able to find our services, hours, location, and contact information easily.

Conformance Status

Our goal is for this website to conform to the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 (opens in a new tab), Level AA. These guidelines explain how to make web content more accessible for people with a wide range of disabilities, and are the standard commonly referenced for the Americans with Disabilities Act (ADA) and California's Unruh Civil Rights Act.

Measures We Have Taken

  • Text alternatives for all meaningful images
  • Color contrast that meets or exceeds WCAG AA requirements
  • A logical heading structure and labeled page regions for screen reader navigation
  • Full keyboard access, with a visible focus indicator and a "Skip to main content" link
  • Links that are identifiable without relying on color, and notices for links that open in a new tab
  • A layout that adapts to small screens and to text zoom up to 200%
  • Respect for the "reduce motion" setting on your device

Assessment Approach

We reviewed this website against the WCAG 2.2 Level AA success criteria using the WebAIM WCAG checklist, along with keyboard-only testing, contrast measurement, and testing at narrow screen widths.

Known Limitations

  • Embedded Google Map. The map on our Contact section is provided by Google, and we cannot fully control its accessibility. The same information is available as text on the page: our address is 8055 Alondra Blvd, Paramount, CA 90723, and the "Open in Maps" link provides directions.

Compatibility

This website is designed to work with current versions of major web browsers and with assistive technologies such as screen readers (for example, VoiceOver, NVDA, and JAWS) and screen magnifiers. It uses standard HTML and CSS and does not require JavaScript.

Feedback and Assistance

If you have difficulty using any part of this website, or would like the information in a different format, please let us know. We welcome your feedback and will work to provide the information you need and to fix the problem.
